# 💜 NEXUS MENU

A custom Roblox Lua UI featuring a sleek purple interface, multiple tabs, player utilities, visual effects, and customizable settings.

> **NEXUS MENU** — Your game, your interface, your style.

## ✨ Features

- 💜 **Custom Purple UI** — A dark theme with purple accents.
- 🏠 **Dashboard** — Quick access to menu features.
- 👤 **Player Tab** — Player-related settings and utilities.
- 👁️ **Spectator Tab** — Player viewing tools.
- 🌎 **World Tab** — World-related utilities.
- ⚙️ **Settings** — Customize your menu experience.
- 🎮 **Controls** — View and configure available keybinds.
- 🎨 **Disco Accent** — Cycle through colorful menu accents.
- 🎉 **Confetti Cannon** — Create colorful particle effects.
- 🪶 **Low Gravity Effect** — Experiment with floatier movement on your character.
- 🌀 **Spin Mode** — Make your character spin.
- 🌈 **Rainbow Trail** — Add a colorful trail effect.
- 📳 **Screen Shake** — Trigger a brief camera effect.
- 😂 **Funny Messages** — Display random in-menu messages.

## ⚠️ Notes

- This project is designed for development and testing in Roblox experiences you own or are authorized to modify.
- Client-side effects may be limited or overridden by the Roblox server.
- Test all features in Roblox Studio before publishing your game.
